STAR Method Blueprint
1. S - Situation (15-20%)

Set context clearly. State the business stakes, team composition, and initial problem.

2. T - Task (10-15%)

Identify what you specifically were tasked to solve, and the timeline constraints.

3. A - Action (50%)

The core of your answer. Detail your personal technical decisions, architectural trade-offs, and execution steps.

4. R - Result (15-20%)

Conclude with hard numbers: latency reduction %, revenue impact, uptime, or team delivery milestones.

Speech & Delivery Benchmarks
  • Ideal Spoken Cadence: 110 – 160 WPM
  • Target Answer Duration: 90 – 180 Seconds
  • Filler Words Ratio: < 2% of words
  • Pronoun Usage: Favor "I" over "We"
